ref: CROR SPEEDS
Q: Match each speed with its definition.
L: Slow speed = A speed not exceeding 15 MPH.
L: Reduced speed = A speed that will permit stopping within one half the range of vision of equipment.
L: Restricted speed = Stopping within one half the range of vision of equipment, prepared to stop short of a switch not properly lined, not exceeding slow speed.
L: Medium speed = A speed not exceeding 30 MPH.
