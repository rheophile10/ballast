Q: Match each term with its definition.
L: Reduced speed = A speed that will permit stopping within one half the range of vision of equipment.
L: Restricted speed = A speed that will permit stopping within one half the range of vision of equipment, prepared to stop short of a switch not properly lined, and in no case exceeding slow speed.
L: Transfer = An engine with or without cars operating on the main track at speeds not exceeding 15 MPH.
